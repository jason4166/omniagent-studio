# Annual leave

ANNUAL-LEAVE policy: Full-time employees receive 10 paid annual-leave days after completing one year of service.

# Salary schedule

SALARY-SCHEDULE policy: Monthly salary is deposited on the final working day of each month.

# Health insurance

HEALTH-PLAN policy: Employee medical insurance starts on the first day of the month after enrollment is approved.

# Refund keyword routing

REFUND-PROOF required evidence and refund approval code are customer-support topics. HR staff must not answer them from this knowledge base.
