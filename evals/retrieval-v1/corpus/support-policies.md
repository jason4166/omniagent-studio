# Refund deadline

REFUND-30D policy: Customers may request a refund within 30 calendar days after delivery. Requests submitted after day 30 are outside the standard refund window.

# Refund evidence

REFUND-PROOF policy: Required evidence includes the order number, payment receipt, and damaged-item photos when damage is claimed.

# Express shipping

EXPRESS-SLA policy: Standard express shipping takes 3 to 5 business days after the warehouse accepts the order.

# Hardware warranty

WARRANTY-24M policy: Hardware is covered for 24 months from the purchase date. Accidental damage is excluded.

# Enterprise password reset

SSO-RESET policy: Enterprise users must ask their organization administrator to reset a password through the single sign-on system.

# Tax invoice

VAT-INVOICE policy: A value-added tax invoice can be requested within 90 days after payment by providing the billing title and tax number.

# Payroll keyword routing

PAYROLL-DATE exact disbursement schedule is an HR topic. Customer support must not answer it from this knowledge base.
